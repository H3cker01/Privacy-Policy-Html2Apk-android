Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 30, 2026

html2apk ("the App") is developed by H3cker01. This Privacy Policy explains what data is collected when you use the App and how it is handled.

1. Information We Collect

When you use html2apk, the following data is processed:

  • HTML files and code you upload — used solely to build your APK and discarded after the build completes.
  • App configuration inputs (app name, package name, permissions, signing options) — used only during the build process.
  • Icon images you upload — processed to generate app icons and discarded after the build.
  • Keystore files (if you choose "Use My Key") — used only to sign your APK and never stored persistently.

2. Build Data Retention

APK artifacts generated by the build pipeline are stored temporarily as GitHub Actions artifacts with a retention period of 1 day, after which they are automatically deleted. We do not retain your HTML, icon, or keystore files beyond the build process.

3. Advertising

The App uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. These networks may collect certain data to serve relevant ads in accordance with their own privacy policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy

We do not sell your personal information to advertisers or any third parties.

4. Local Storage

Build history is stored locally on your device and is never transmitted to our servers. You can clear it at any time from within the app.

5. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The date at the top reflects the most recent revision. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

7. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, reach us via the H3cker01 GitHub profile.
